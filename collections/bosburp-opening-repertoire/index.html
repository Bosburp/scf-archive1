ABOUT US

We're a community dedicated to creating and sharing original, high-quality work, completely free and accessible to all.

On Lichess, many excellent community studies can be difficult to find. Search results often bring unannotated or low-effort studies to the top, while high-quality, educational work remains buried. At the same time, the number of likes doesn't always reflect true quality, allowing copied or less meaningful content to gain visibility.

Our community was created to address this. We aim to support high-quality creators and promote work that offers real value. Every study we share has been reviewed, with the goal of making high-quality content easier to find and appreciate.

SUBMIT A STUDY

We are always looking for high-quality, educational chess studies to add to our archive.

How to contribute:

All submissions are handled via our Discord server to ensure a proper review process.

  1. Join our Discord Server.
  2. Navigate to the #study-submissions channel.
  3. Follow the pinned template to post your link.
